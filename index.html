Privacy Policy — Sandman & Co.

Effective 20 October 2026. Applies to the Android app Sandman & Co. (package com.inatasunsoft.sandmanco), published by Inata Sun Soft.

Sandman & Co. is a single-player idle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not upload your save file anywhere. This policy explains what stays on your device and the small amount of data that the services inside the app handle.

What the app itself collects

No personal information. Your progress (dream dust, machines, crew, town, story, Dream Realm stage, settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Ads only ever play when you tap a "Watch video" button.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the services above, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your rights and how to request data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app stores is on your device and is removed when you uninstall it. For data held by AdMob or Firebase Analytics, reset your Advertising ID or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Purchase history is kept by Google Play in your Google account.
